No AI

In a world where we are constantly bombarded by content ceated by and promoting Artificial Intelligence and the tools therewithin.

I stand firmly against the use and proliferation of AI as a concept, content created with AI, tools which employ AI and any and all promotion of any AI concept, content created with AI or tools which employ AI.

I, Queekusme/Annabelle Kennedy hereby reject AI and all of its associated accoutrements forthwith, and will do my part to educate on the harms AI can and will cause in all aspects of its use.

Before considering using AI yourself, please educate yourself on the harms it will cause, such as but not limited to: The Environment, human content creators and your own personal development [1].

This pledge stands from today until my final breath. It may be updated to accomodate for changing terminology/usage however its core meaning maintains. Please review any changes in the git log for the repository which holds my website at https://github.com/queekusme/queekusme.github.com

Signed Queekusme/Annabelle Kennedy.

[1] I have been living and learning without AI and consider myself all the better for it. I do not live to require AI assistance and due to this absence believe I can create honest conent free from bias or external influence outsie of my own personal experiences.
