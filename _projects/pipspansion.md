My first hexpansion for the Tildagon Badge for EMFCamp<cite data-id="emfcamp" />, a bit of a spur of the moment design because I wanted something to add to my badge when I get it. I decided upon doing the Star Trek Rank Pips because of the theme for 2026 being 'Space'<cite data-id="emf_theme" />. I've always enjoyed Star Trek and so thought this would go hand-hand with everything else.

The design includes 5 pips for full customisation options allowing for 4 full pips 'Captain' as well as lower ranks of lesser numbers of pips including the partial ranks indicated by the black centered pip.

The design was modelled in OpenSCAD against the 2026 badge STEP files<cite data-id="tilde_step" /> in order to make it sit flush against the faceplate of the badge. The badge design has changed slightly since 2022 increasing the height, which appears to be the design going forward.

I’m not selling any of these, however if anyone is interested in making their own I can pop the .3mf file up somewhere. Message me on Mastodon<cite data-id="mastodon" /> if you are interested.

As soon as I have my badge I'll add an image above of the hexpansion attached to the badge.
